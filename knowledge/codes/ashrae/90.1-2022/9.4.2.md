Each hotel and motel guest room must have at least one master control at the main entrance that controls all permanently installed luminaires and switched receptacles. This master control must automatically turn off all loads within 20 minutes of the guest leaving the room, which may be accomplished by a key card switch, door sensor, or occupancy sensor. All bathroom lighting must be controlled separately from the bedroom.
