The Building Area Method allows a single LPD allowance applied to the entire building gross square footage. It is simpler but typically less flexible than the Space-by-Space Method. The total LPD cannot exceed the value in Table 9.6.1 for the building type. If a building contains multiple uses, the building type is the predominant use by floor area.
