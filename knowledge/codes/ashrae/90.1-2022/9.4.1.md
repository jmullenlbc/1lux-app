All interior lighting in buildings larger than 5,000 sq ft must have automatic shutoff controls. Acceptable methods include: (1) occupancy sensor that turns lights off within 30 minutes of occupancy ceasing, (2) scheduled automatic shutoff that turns lights off at specific times, (3) signal from another control or building automation system. Occupancy sensors must also comply: in spaces where automatic partial-off is used instead of full shutoff, lights must reduce to at least 50% power within 30 minutes of vacancy.
