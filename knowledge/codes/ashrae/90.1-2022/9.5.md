The Space-by-Space Method calculates the interior lighting power allowance by multiplying the LPD allowance for each space type by the area of that space. The total allowance is the sum of all space allowances. This method is preferred when a building has many different space types. LPD allowances are listed in Table 9.5.1. Spaces not listed must use the LPD of the most similar space type or default to 0.82 W/ft² (office).
