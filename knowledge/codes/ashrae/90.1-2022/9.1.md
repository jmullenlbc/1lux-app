Section 9 applies to interior and exterior lighting systems in all buildings covered by the standard. Lighting power limits apply to all permanently installed lighting, including general illumination, decorative lighting, and lighting in mechanical rooms and stairwells. Exceptions include emergency lighting that operates only on backup power, lighting in spaces specifically exempted by Section 9.2, and lighting in dwelling units.
