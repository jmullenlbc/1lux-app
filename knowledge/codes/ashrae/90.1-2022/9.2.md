The following lighting is exempt from the interior lighting power limits: (1) emergency lighting that has dual-switching capability operating only on emergency backup power, (2) decorative gas lighting systems, (3) lighting in dwelling units, (4) lighting equipment that is for sale or being demonstrated in lighting showrooms, (5) lighting equipment used in agricultural applications. Note: Exit signs are not exempt from the standard even though they are required by life safety codes.
