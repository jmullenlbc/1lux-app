Daylight-responsive controls are required in: (1) all primary sidelighted areas of 120 sf or more where the total installed lighting power is 150 W or more, (2) all daylit areas under skylights of 900 sf or more where the total installed lighting power is 150 W or more, (3) all primary sidelighted areas in addition to those in item 1 that are 900 sf or more. Daylight controls must reduce electric lighting by at least 50% or maintain illuminance set point within 10% under sufficient daylight conditions.
