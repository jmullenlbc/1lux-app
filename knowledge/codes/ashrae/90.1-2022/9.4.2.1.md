All exterior lighting must be controlled by a combination of a photosensor and a time switch, or an astronomical time switch. The photosensor must turn lights on at dusk and off at dawn. The time switch provides additional control for reducing lighting during late-night hours. Façade and landscape lighting must be turned off between midnight and 6 AM or when the building is not in use, whichever is later.
