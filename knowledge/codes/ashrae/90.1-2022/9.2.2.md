All interior lighting must be capable of being reduced to at least 50% power through any of the following methods: (1) daylight-responsive controls, (2) occupancy/vacancy sensing, (3) manual continuous dimming, (4) multi-level switching (at least 2 steps where one is ≤50%). Exceptions: single-occupant spaces controlled by occupancy sensor, spaces where multi-level would endanger safety, and emergency lighting.
