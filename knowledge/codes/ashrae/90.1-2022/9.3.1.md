Additional LPD allowances are permitted for: (1) additional 1.0 W/ft² for retail display lighting beyond the base space allowance, (2) hotel and motel guest rooms get an additional 0.35 W/ft² when supplemental heat lamps are installed in bathrooms, (3) specific task lighting connected to switched receptacles (less than 0.3 W/ft²) may be excluded if it is plug-in and not permanently installed. Supplemental task lighting at workstations is included in the LPD calculation when it is hardwired.
