Decorative and display lighting, including case lighting, and lighting in hotel lobbies, must be separately switched from general illumination and must be controlled by a time-of-day schedule or occupancy sensor. This allows decorative lighting to be turned off during periods of no occupancy while maintaining general illumination if needed.
