Exterior lighting power is calculated using a base allowance plus tradeable and non-tradeable allowances based on exterior zones (E1-E4 per ASHRAE/IESNA). The total exterior LPD is limited based on the lighting zone designation of the site. Trade-off between exterior zones is not permitted. Non-tradeable allowances apply to specific features such as vehicle canopies, walkways, sales lots, and building facades.
