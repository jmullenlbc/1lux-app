Occupancy sensors are required in the following space types: offices 250 sf or smaller, classrooms and lecture halls, conference/meeting rooms, employee lunch and break rooms, storage rooms, janitorial closets, restrooms, locker rooms. For spaces 250 sf or smaller the sensor must control all lighting in the space. Manual-on sensors (vacancy sensors) are preferred over auto-on in private offices and other enclosed spaces where occupants can manually turn on lights.
