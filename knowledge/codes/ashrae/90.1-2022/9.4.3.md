All exit signs must use LED sources or equivalent with a maximum of 5 watts per face. Illuminated exit signs that use more than 5 W per face are not compliant. This applies to internally illuminated exit signs; externally illuminated exit signs are exempt from the 5W limit but must comply with IBC requirements. Exit signs in compliance with this section do not count toward the building's interior LPD allowance.
