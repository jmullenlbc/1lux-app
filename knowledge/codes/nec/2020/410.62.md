Track lighting must be installed so that the maximum load on any 1-foot section of track does not exceed the ratings of the section. The total connected load of track lighting must not exceed the current rating of the branch circuit to which the track is connected. Luminaires on track may not be installed within the zone of a bathtub or shower. Track lighting in other areas must have a grounding conductor. The total wattage of luminaires on a track must be calculated at 150% of actual load for branch circuit sizing.
