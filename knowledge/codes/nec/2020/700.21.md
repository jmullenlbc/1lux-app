Dimmer controls are not permitted on emergency lighting branch circuits unless the dimmer is specifically listed for use in emergency lighting applications and the luminaire can revert to full brightness automatically upon activation of the emergency power source. Standard dimmers connected to emergency lighting circuits will prevent the emergency luminaires from operating at required levels during a power outage. Dimming systems must be designed to bypass the dimming function during emergency mode.
