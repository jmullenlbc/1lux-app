Recessed luminaires must not be installed in direct contact with thermal insulation unless listed for that purpose (marked 'IC' for insulation contact). Non-IC-rated recessed luminaires must maintain a minimum 3-inch clearance from insulation and a minimum 0.5-inch clearance from combustible materials. Recessed luminaires must be thermally protected unless the fixture is labeled Type IC. Installation above suspended ceilings requires the fixture to be listed for installation in contact with insulation if insulation will be placed above the plenum.
