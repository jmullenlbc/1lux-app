Only surface-mounted or recessed incandescent (not allowed due to closet clearance requirements — see below), fluorescent, or LED luminaires with completely enclosed light sources are permitted in clothes closets. Permitted locations: surface-mounted or recessed luminaires on the ceiling if there is a minimum 12-inch clearance horizontally from the nearest point of the storage space. Surface-mounted fluorescent or LED on the wall above the door are permitted only if there is a minimum 6-inch clearance from storage. Completely enclosed luminaires must be used to prevent ignition of stored materials.
