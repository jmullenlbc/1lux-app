Unit equipment (emergency battery packs) must be permanently fixed in place, with batteries and lamps of the same type, have sufficient wattage to maintain required illumination for 1.5 hours, have an automatic transfer switch to battery power upon normal power failure, have a test switch accessible only to authorized personnel, and be listed under UL 924. The unit equipment must provide at least 1 foot-candle average illumination at floor level. Remote lamp heads must be connected to the unit equipment as permitted by the listing.
