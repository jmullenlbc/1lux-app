Acceptable power sources for emergency systems include: (1) storage batteries (lead acid, nickel-cadmium, lithium, or other approved type) that automatically provide power with no delay, (2) generator sets driven by prime movers — must start and transfer load within 10 seconds, (3) uninterruptible power supplies (UPS) that provide immediate power and sustain for required duration, (4) self-contained unit equipment (battery packs) listed to UL 924. Central battery systems must supply all emergency loads for at least 1.5 hours (90 minutes). Unit equipment battery packs must supply the connected load for at least 1.5 hours.
