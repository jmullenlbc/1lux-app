The Critical Branch supplies task illumination in specific locations: critical care areas, intensive care units (ICU), coronary care units (CCU), operating rooms, anesthesia work areas, medication preparation areas, nurses' stations, telephone equipment rooms, and generator equipment rooms. The Critical Branch also supplies selected receptacles throughout the patient care areas. Lighting on the Critical Branch must be automatically restored within 10 seconds of normal power failure.
