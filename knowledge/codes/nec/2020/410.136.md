Branch circuits for lighting must comply with Article 210. Each luminaire must be connected to a grounded (neutral) conductor and an ungrounded (hot) conductor. The switch controlling a luminaire must be in the ungrounded (hot) conductor only. Luminaires must not be used to supply loads other than lighting unless the luminaire is specifically designed and marked for additional loads. The weight of a luminaire must not exceed the box or support capacity.
