In healthcare occupancies (Group I-2), the Life Safety Branch must supply: exit signs, exit illumination, patient room corridor lighting, stairway lighting, generator and transfer switch lighting, and illuminated panic hardware. The Life Safety Branch is the most critical branch of the essential electrical system and must receive power within 10 seconds of normal power failure. Lighting on the Life Safety Branch must be permanently fixed and not on dimmer controls or occupancy sensors that could turn off the light.
