All luminaires and lampholders must be listed. Luminaires installed in wet locations must be listed for wet locations and marked 'Suitable for Wet Locations.' Luminaires in damp locations (covered porches, under canopies, carports) must be listed for damp or wet locations and marked 'Suitable for Damp Locations.' A luminaire marked only for dry locations cannot be installed in damp or wet areas. Listed luminaires are typically marked with UL, ETL, CSA, or similar third-party listing marks.
