Article 411 covers lighting systems, including luminaires and transformers or power supplies, that operate at 30 volts or less and are used for outdoor decorative lighting and residential and commercial applications. Systems must be listed and the transformer or power supply must be listed for the intended application. Listed systems with Class 2 power supplies have relaxed wiring requirements. The power supply must have integral protection to interrupt current in case of fault. Outdoor installations must use cable listed for direct burial or must be in conduit.
