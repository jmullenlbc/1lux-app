Article 410 covers luminaires (light fixtures), lampholders, pendants, incandescent filament lamps, arc lamps, electric discharge lamps, decorative lighting products, and all wiring and equipment forming part of such products or installations. Luminaires must be listed and labeled for their intended use and environment (dry, damp, or wet locations). Installation must comply with the luminaire's listing and the manufacturer's instructions.
