Article 700 covers electrical safety and emergency operations of electrical systems, circuits, and equipment installed for illumination or power in the event the normal supply fails. Emergency systems must supply power to exit lights, emergency lighting, fire detection and alarm systems, public address systems, and other systems required for evacuation. Emergency systems must be legally required by municipal, state, federal, or other codes. Legally Required Standby Systems (Article 701) and Optional Standby Systems (Article 702) are separate categories.
