In bathtub and shower areas, no part of cord-connected luminaires, hanging luminaires, lighting track, pendants, or ceiling fans shall be located within a zone measured 3 feet horizontally and 8 feet vertically from the top of the bathtub rim or shower stall threshold. Luminaires within this zone must be listed for damp locations (or wet locations if subject to shower spray). GFCI protection is required for all luminaires within 6 feet of the edge of a bathtub or shower stall.
