The voltage between conductors in circuits supplying luminaires must not exceed: 120 volts (nominal) for screw-shell lampholders in dwelling units, hotel/motel guest rooms, or similar occupancies. 277 volts (nominal) allowed for fluorescent and LED luminaires in commercial, industrial, and institutional locations. 480 volts allowed for industrial manufacturing buildings with qualified maintenance personnel. 480/277V systems are common in commercial office and retail applications; 120/208V is typical in residential and smaller commercial buildings.
