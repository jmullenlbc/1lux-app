Occupancy sensors are mandatory in: classrooms and lecture halls, conference/meeting rooms, employee lunch and break rooms, private offices, restrooms, storage rooms (100 sf or smaller), janitorial closets. For spaces with only one occupant (private offices), vacancy sensors (manual-on, auto-off) are preferred. Sensors in corridors, open offices, and large spaces may use partial-off (dim to 20% or less) in lieu of full shutoff, provided the space returns to full power when reoccupied.
