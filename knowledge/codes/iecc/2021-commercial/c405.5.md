Exterior lighting power allowances are based on the lighting zone of the site (LZ1 through LZ4) per IESNA RP-33. Base allowances per lighting zone: LZ1 (rural, dark): 50% reduction from standard. LZ2 (residential): standard base. LZ3 (commercial/suburban): 125% of base. LZ4 (high commercial activity): 150% of base. Additionally, uncovered vehicle sales lots get 2.4 W/ft² for display. Covered canopies (gas stations, drop-off): 1.25 W/ft². Walkways under 10 ft wide: 1.0 W/linear ft.
