Each hotel and motel guest room must have a control device at the main entry that automatically turns off all HVAC and lighting within 30 minutes of the guest leaving the room (as detected by door switch, key card, or occupancy sensor). All bathroom lighting must be separately switched from bedroom lighting. All decorative and accent lighting in the guest room must be able to be turned off by the master control. A means must be provided to override the master-off for maintenance personnel.
