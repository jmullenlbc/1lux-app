Daylight-responsive automatic controls are required for: (1) all enclosed spaces with windows or skylights where the window area exceeds 150 sf or skylight area exceeds 900 sf, (2) all enclosed spaces that are adjacent to exterior windows where the sidelighted area is at least 120 sf and has a window-to-wall ratio of 40% or more. Controls must maintain illuminance to within 10% of the design set point, or reduce lighting to 50% or less when sufficient daylight is available. Photosensors must be separately zoned for each exposure (north, south, east, west).
