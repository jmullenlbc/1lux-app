The IECC adopts the lighting power allowances from ASHRAE 90.1 by reference. Buildings must comply with either the Space-by-Space method (Table C405.4.2(1)) or the Building Area method (Table C405.4.2(2)). The IECC tables match ASHRAE 90.1-2019 values; states adopting IECC 2021 may amend the tables. Additional LPD allowances are available for: retail display lighting (+1.0 W/ft²), ornamental/special-effects lighting in performing arts venues, and task lighting at individual workstations that is controlled independently.
