In the residential provisions (R-2 multifamily common areas subject to commercial code), at least 75% of the lamps in permanently installed fixtures must have an efficacy of 45 lumens per watt or greater. The remaining 25% may use lower-efficacy sources. This applies to all common areas including corridors, lobbies, stairwells, laundry rooms, and amenity spaces. Individual dwelling units are covered by the residential IECC (IRC) provisions, not the commercial provisions.
