All exterior lighting must be controlled by a combination photosensor/time switch or an astronomically timed control that turns lighting on at dusk and off at dawn. Additionally, exterior building façade lighting and exterior landscape/accent lighting must be capable of being turned off or reduced by at least 50% between midnight and 6 AM, or when business is closed, whichever is later. Exemptions: lighting for safety and security, lighting for signs (covered by separate section), and lighting required by a regulatory agency.
