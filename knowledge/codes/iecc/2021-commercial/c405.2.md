Interior lighting in commercial buildings must be equipped with automatic controls capable of turning off all lighting in each space independently. Automatic shutoff is required for all buildings regardless of size. Acceptable methods: (1) occupancy sensor that turns off within 20 minutes of vacancy (IECC is slightly more stringent than ASHRAE 90.1's 30-minute requirement), (2) time-of-day schedule operating no longer than 2 hours past normal business hours, (3) signal from another control system or building management system. Manual override switches must allow overriding the automatic shutoff for no more than 2 hours.
