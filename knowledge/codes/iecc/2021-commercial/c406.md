Buildings may meet IECC requirements by selecting from a menu of energy efficiency measures. Lighting-related credits include: (1) Enhanced interior lighting controls: occupancy sensors in all spaces including corridors and open offices with partial-off capability, earning 2 compliance points. (2) Daylighting with sidelighting: installing continuous dimming daylight controls for at least 50% of the connected interior lighting load, earning 2 points. (3) High-efficacy luminaires: installing lighting with a system efficacy of 60 lm/W or more throughout 90% of spaces, earning 1 point.
