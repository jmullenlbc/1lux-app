Emergency lighting is required in: (1) aisles, corridors, and exit passageways in Group A, B, E, F, H, I, M, R-1, R-2, and S occupancies, (2) extended detention and mental health facilities, (3) underground and windowless structures, (4) assembly areas, (5) interior exit stairways and ramps, (6) interior exit access stairways and ramps, (7) electrical rooms requiring over 600V service, (8) corridors serving an occupant load of 30 or more persons in residential occupancies. Note: sprinklered Group B offices under 10 persons and 1,000 sf are exempt.
