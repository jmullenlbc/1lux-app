The power supply for required emergency lighting must be provided by one of the following: (1) battery-powered unit equipment listed for emergency use complying with UL 924, (2) on-site generator complying with Chapter 27 of the IBC, (3) a separate uninterruptible power supply (UPS), (4) separate storage batteries directly connected to emergency lighting circuits. The emergency power supply must supply power for at least 90 minutes and must be fully automatic in activation.
