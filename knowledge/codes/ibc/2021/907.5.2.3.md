In sleeping units, visible notification appliances (strobes) must be installed in compliance with NFPA 72. For hotel and motel guest rooms, a strobe must be provided in the room. The candela output for sleeping area strobes must comply with NFPA 72 Table 18.5.5.4.1(a) based on room dimensions. Strobes in corridors used as sleeping unit paths must be spaced per NFPA 72. Candela ratings and placement must be coordinated between the fire alarm and lighting designers.
