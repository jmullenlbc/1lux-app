Exit signs are required at: (1) every exit door from a story, (2) where two or more exits or exit access doors are required, at each exit or exit access door, (3) where exit access corridors or aisles are required, at each point where a corridor path intersects with another corridor or changes direction. Exit signs must be located where the exit or exit access door is not readily visible from the egress path. Not required in Main Exits of Group A with 300 or fewer occupants, Group I-1, and residential Group R occupancies with 4 or fewer stories.
