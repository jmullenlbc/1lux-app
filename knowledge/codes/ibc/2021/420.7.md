In hotel and motel guest rooms (Group R-1) and apartment units (Group R-2), nighttime illumination of egress paths must be provided. A night light or low-level lighting that automatically activates when the main room lighting is turned off must illuminate the path from sleeping areas to bathroom facilities. Occupant-controlled dimmers on exit path lighting are acceptable if set to maintain a minimum of 0.1 foot-candle at floor level.
