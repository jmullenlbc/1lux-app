Every habitable room must have natural light through windows or skylights with a minimum glazing area of not less than 8% of the floor area of the room. Where natural light cannot be provided, artificial light providing not less than 6 foot-candles of average maintained illuminance at a height of 30 inches above the floor is acceptable as an alternative. This applies to offices (Group B), residential (Group R), and care occupancies (Group I).
