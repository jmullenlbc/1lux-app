Required egress doors must be distinguishable from the adjacent construction. Mirrors and highly reflective surfaces shall not be installed on or adjacent to any required egress door where they may confuse occupants. Exit access doors in a Group I-2 occupancy used for sleeping units must have illuminated hardware or a lighted room number adjacent to the door to allow identification from the corridor during emergency conditions.
