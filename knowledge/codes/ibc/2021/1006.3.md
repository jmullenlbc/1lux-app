Emergency lighting must provide an average illumination of at least 1 foot-candle (10 lux) at the walking surface and not less than 0.1 foot-candle (1 lux) at any point during the required 90-minute duration. The ratio of maximum to minimum illumination must not exceed 40:1. Initial emergency lighting level immediately after failure must be at least 1 fc average for the first 10 seconds. After this initial period, it may reduce to 0.6 fc average and 0.06 fc minimum, but only if the space is being evacuated.
