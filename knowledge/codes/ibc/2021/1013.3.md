Exit signs must be internally or externally illuminated. The face of an exit sign must be illuminated to a level of not less than 5 foot-candles (54 lux) by a self-contained power source or connected to the emergency lighting circuit. Internally illuminated exit signs must have a minimum luminance of 25 foot-lamberts (86 cd/m²). LED exit signs are permitted and must meet UL 924 listing requirements.
