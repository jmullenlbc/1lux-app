Exit signs must be connected to the building's emergency power supply. Signs must remain lit during normal and emergency power conditions. The power supply for exit signs must be capable of maintaining the required illumination for at least 90 minutes in case of primary power failure. Approved self-luminous signs that provide continuous illumination independent of external power sources are permitted in lieu of electrically illuminated signs.
