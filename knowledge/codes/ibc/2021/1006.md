The means of egress must be illuminated at all times the building is occupied. Required illumination includes all exit access corridors, exit corridors, exit discharge, and exit passageways. The minimum illuminance at the walking surface must be not less than 1 foot-candle (10 lux). For sleeping units in Groups I-1, I-2, I-3, R-1, and R-2, the illuminance may be reduced to 0.2 foot-candles during nighttime hours. Emergency lighting must be capable of maintaining the required 1 fc for at least 90 minutes after a power outage.
