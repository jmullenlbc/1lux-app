Parking garages open to the outside (open parking structures) are exempt from interior lighting power limits under ASHRAE 90.1 but must comply with minimum illumination requirements. Enclosed parking garages must provide minimum 1 fc average at floor level in all driving aisles, with not less than 0.5 fc at any point. Ramps must provide a minimum of 5 fc at the top and bottom of each ramp and 2 fc along the ramp length.
