Accessible means of egress shall be provided in accordance with this section. Accessible means of egress shall be illuminated as required for the means of egress in accordance with Section 1006. Areas of refuge shall be provided with a two-way communication system complying with Sections 1009.8.1 and 1009.8.2. The illumination requirements of Section 1006 apply to all accessible egress routes including elevator lobbies used as areas of refuge, horizontal exits, and exterior areas for assisted rescue.

Stairways that are part of the accessible means of egress shall comply with all means of egress illumination requirements. Exterior areas for assisted rescue shall be illuminated to a minimum of 1 foot-candle at the ground level at all times the building is occupied.
