The power supply for means of egress illumination shall normally be provided by the premises' electrical supply. In the event of failure of normal electrical supply, illumination shall be automatically provided from an emergency system for the following occupancies where such occupancies require two or more means of egress: Group A having an occupant load of more than 50 persons, Group B, Group E, Group F, Group H, Group I, Group M, Group R-1, Group R-2, Group S-1, and Group U occupancies.

Emergency power systems shall be supplied from storage batteries, unit equipment, or an on-site generator. The emergency power system shall provide power for a duration of not less than 90 minutes and shall consist of storage batteries, unit equipment, or a generator. The installation of the emergency power system shall be in accordance with NFPA 70 (NEC) Article 700. Unit equipment shall be permanently fixed in place including the battery, charger, and luminaire. Unit equipment shall be listed for the application.
