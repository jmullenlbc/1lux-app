The means of egress shall be illuminated at all times the building space served by the means of egress is occupied. The illumination shall be continuous. Means of egress illumination level shall not be less than 1 foot-candle (11 lux) at the walking surface level. For the purposes of this section, means of egress shall include aisles, corridors, ramps and stairways leading to an exit and the exit itself. In sleeping units of Groups R-1, R-2, I-1, and I-2, the means of egress illumination shall be permitted to be controlled by automatic means. The minimum illuminance level in sleeping areas during nighttime hours shall not be less than 0.2 foot-candle (2.2 lux) at the floor level.
